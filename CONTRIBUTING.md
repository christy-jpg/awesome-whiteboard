# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful.

## What belongs here

- **Maintained.** Some sign of life in the last year or so.
- **Genuinely useful** for building or using an infinite canvas / whiteboard.
- **Accurately licensed.** State the real license. "Source-available" is not "open source", and this list says so plainly in both directions.

## Entry format

One line, in the section it fits:

```
- [Name](https://link) — License. One or two sentences on what it is and, honestly, where it fits less well.
```

Write the tradeoff, not the marketing. An entry that only lists strengths is less useful than one that tells a reader when to pick something else.

## Corrections

Licenses and pricing change. If an entry here is out of date or unfair, open an issue or a PR — corrections take priority over additions.

## Self-promotion

Adding your own project is fine and encouraged, as long as it meets the bar above and your description is as honest about limitations as it is about features.
